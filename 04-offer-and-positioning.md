## 2. Positioning chain and decision

1. **Genuine alternatives in this situation:** established reformas firms with fixed-price packages and long track records (Selarom, A.B.R.); premium design-build firms (Koncepto); quote platforms (Cronoshare, Habitissimo); loose tradespeople found via referrals or Facebook groups, sometimes overseen by a property manager; an architect plus a separate contractor. (02 §2)
2. **What differs:** (a) open-book quotes including the margin; (b) change approval in writing before execution; (c) an explicit single workmanship and protection standard regardless of finish level; (d) a small, founder-led operation with a 24-hour reply and direct contact.
3. **Value created:** the owner can compare and understand the price, stays in control of extras, and can live through the works with the home protected. This targets the two frictions found in research: price growth after vague agreements and loss of control over the timeline (02 Cards 1, 3).
4. **Who cares most:** owner-occupiers doing partial or phased renovations (disruption matters most), and international residents who find contractors hard to reach and communicate with (02 Card 2, S6).
5. **Market frame:** "renovation company" (empresa de reformas), a familiar frame, qualified as *open-book and supervised*. It is not presented as a design studio or architect.
6. **Proof available and needed:** available: documented process, sample quote structure, company identity. Needed: real Barcelona projects, client feedback, guarantee terms, insurance confirmation.

**Positioning decision (Decision; Test):** Reform by Amir is a Barcelona renovation company for people improving the home they live in, and it competes on openness and supervision rather than price or years in business. Its quotes show exactly what each euro covers, including the margin. Changes happen only with written approval, and the same careful execution applies whether the client chooses economical, mid-range or premium materials.

## 6. Fit boundary and rejected alternatives

**Good fit:** owners who want to understand their costs, value careful work in an occupied home, and prefer a supervised, single point of responsibility, from one room to a full apartment.

**Not a fit (Hypothesis, to be stated gently on the site):** people choosing on lowest price alone; people wanting a firm fixed price or finish date without a visit; people wanting cash or "sin IVA" work; people who only need a single tradesperson for a very small repair (check with the founder before publishing a minimum job size).

**Rejected alternatives:**
- *Lead with integral reforms for new buyers (audience A):* more competition, needs a track record; kept in scope but not the lead message (02 §4).
- *Compete on price or fixed-price packages:* conflicts with the founder boundary and the low market anchors.
- *Lead with interior design or energy efficiency:* no research signal and proof not ready; kept as optional services.
- *Portfolio of Iran projects:* founder decision to focus on the Spanish market; the experience may be mentioned factually in the founder bio if the founder wishes.
- *Showroom visit as a CTA:* not yet open.

## 7. Validation mechanism, risks, falsifying evidence, open questions

**Mechanism:** the website experiment from 02 §11. Estimator → "Request a free home visit" form → reply within 24 h through the chosen method → visit → itemised quote → signed or lost. The founder keeps a follow-up log with the outcome and the reason when a lead is lost.

**Largest risks:**
1. *Viability:* fair-value, open-book pricing may lose to low anchors. Falsified if most quotes are rejected for price.
2. *Desirability:* open-book pricing may not matter to buyers. Falsified if people who don't proceed never mention transparency and requests don't reference it.
3. *Trust:* new firm, no portfolio. Falsified if visitors use the estimator but almost none request a visit, or if they ask for references.
4. *Audience:* requests may be mainly integral reforms of empty flats, which would mean a shift to audience A.

**Open questions for `/brand-concept`:**
- Brand name form: "Reform by Amir" versus the email "reformabyamir". Confirm the exact spelling.
- The founder's personal presence (photo, bio, languages spoken).
- Tone across ES / CA / EN.
- How the "inspiration, not completed projects" imagery rule shapes the visual language.
- Whether energy efficiency appears in the brand story or only as a service.

**Open business items (before launch):** own cost data for the estimator; IVA treatment; guarantee terms; insurance; company details for the legal notice; privacy notice for the form (GDPR, Spain's data-protection law LOPDGDD); form-service connection.
