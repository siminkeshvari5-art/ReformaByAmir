## 03. Brand Personality

**Traits** (Inferred from founder statements in 00 §5–8 and 04; approved at the checkpoint):

| Trait | Business behaviour | Communication behaviour | Anti-behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Precise | Measured scope, itemised quote, realistic programme, checklist-based finishing | Concrete nouns and numbers; names what is included and excluded | Vague "high quality", round promises, "approx. everything" |
| Open | Shows materials, labour, taxes and margin; explains what can move price or dates | Explains the *why*; states uncertainty plainly | Hidden extras; "we'll see later"; fine print |
| Respectful | Protects the home, cleans daily, keeps agreed contact method, no repeated sales calls | Polite, calm, never pushy; respects the visitor's decision time | Urgency, pressure, chasing leads, talking down |
| Calm | Plans ahead, anticipates issues, communicates changes early | Even, reassuring tone; no drama about industry horror stories | Fear-selling ("renovation nightmares"), exclamation-heavy copy |

**Desired perception** (Decision — founder 00 §8 and this stage): *"They told us exactly what everything cost, kept us updated, and treated our home with care. It felt professional but personal."*

**Productive tension:** **precise but warm**. Exact about work and numbers, human with people. Every technical detail is paired with what it means for the person living in the home. (Decision — founder, this stage: "clean, precise, trustworthy and professional, but still warm and human")

## 04. Brand Values

All three values are founder commitments (00 §5–8, 04 §1; this stage), not yet evidenced practice in Spain.

| Value | Means | Does not mean | Observable behaviour | Customer implication | Trade-off | Violation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **One standard** | Preparation, alignment, protection and finishing are identical at every finish level | Identical materials or price; that economical finishes look premium | Same workmanship checklist on every job; supervision regardless of budget | A smaller budget still gets careful work | Less margin on low-budget jobs; may decline jobs where the standard can't be met | Rushing installation because the materials are cheap |
| **Every cost visible** | Quote shows materials, labour, taxes and margin; changes priced and approved in writing first | Guaranteeing the final number before inspection; disclosing supplier contracts | Itemised quote; written change orders; cost updates during works | The client can compare and control spending | Harder to compete with headline low prices; more admin | Adding an extra without written approval; an unexplained lump sum |
| **Respect for the home and the agreement** | Treat the occupied home carefully; keep to the agreed programme and contact method; tell early when something changes | Absolute on-time guarantees; unlimited availability | Protection of doors, sockets and surfaces; daily clean-up; agreed update rhythm; reply within 24 h | Less disruption and anxiety | Slower start on some jobs to plan properly | Leaving mess; going silent; repeated sales calls |

## 05. Conceptual Territory

| Field | Content | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Central concept | **The spirit level**: the standard that doesn't change. A level reads the same on economical tiles as on marble. It is the principle behind the purpose line. | Decision — founder, this stage |
| Key idea 1 | **The open ledger**: every line visible. The itemised breakdown (materials, labour, taxes, margin) as a recurring device. | Decision — founder, this stage |
| Key idea 2 | **The careful guest**: how we behave inside someone's home. Protect, keep order, clean, leave it properly finished. | Decision — founder, this stage |
| Key idea 3 | **Choices vary, the care doesn't**: the side-by-side of finish levels with an identical workmanship standard | Inferred from the purpose line |
| Metaphors | (1) The spirit level's centred bubble: "level is level", neutral to price. (2) The ledger line: nothing unaccounted for. | Decision — founder, this stage |
| Cultural references | (1) The craftsman's habit of checking twice (*mesura dues vegades, talla'n una* / measure twice, cut once), a shared trade proverb, not an invented heritage. (2) Barcelona apartment life: occupied flats, neighbours, *comunitat de propietaris*, the respect needed when working in shared buildings. | Inferred; use lightly |

**Hierarchy (founder):** spirit level = principle · open ledger = transparency · careful guest = behaviour.

**Risks and must-not-imitate:** the tool metaphor can feel cold or DIY-store generic, so always pair it with human context. Don't imitate Barcelona Modernisme or tile clichés as fake heritage. Don't borrow luxury design-build codes (Koncepto-style prestige). Don't let the ledger look like an accountancy or fintech brand. No invented history in Barcelona.

## 06. Voice & Tone

**Voice characteristics** (Inferred; approved):
1. **Clear:** short sentences, one idea each, everyday words; technical terms explained once (e.g. *comunicat d'obres*).
2. **Specific:** numbers, lists, named steps and materials instead of adjectives.
3. **Calm and respectful:** no urgency, no fear, no superlatives. Uncertainty is stated with its reason.

**Tone by situation:**

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| First contact / homepage | Warm, direct, reassuring. Lead with the person's home and choices. |
| Estimator and prices | Plain and honest. Ranges, what moves them, "final price after the visit". |
| Uncertainty (hidden conditions, permits, dates) | Matter-of-fact. Name the risk, explain how it's handled and communicated. |
| Form and confirmation | Brief and exact about what happens next and what won't happen (no repeated calls). |
| Change or delay during works (future) | Early, factual, with options and cost in writing. |
| Declining a job / not a fit | Polite and clear, without judgement. |

**Expressions to use:** "Here's what's included, and what isn't." · "Your choice of materials changes the price, not the care." / "Tu elección de materiales cambia el precio, no el cuidado."
**Expressions to avoid:** superlatives ("the best", "the cheapest", "la más limpia de Barcelona"); pressure CTAs ("Book now!", "¡Últimas plazas!", "Oferta por tiempo limitado").

**Do / don't examples:**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| EN: "We'll reply within 24 hours by the method you choose." | EN: "Our team will call you right away!" |
| ES: "Te enseñamos cada coste: materiales, mano de obra, impuestos y nuestro margen." | ES: "Precios imbatibles, sin sorpresas." |
| CA: "El preu final el fixem després de veure el pis i definir la feina." | CA: "Preu tancat garantit des del primer dia." |
| EN: "If we find something hidden behind a wall, we tell you, price it, and wait for your OK." | EN: "No surprises, ever." |

**Constraints:** plain-language reading level; forms and labels accessible; the Spanish register uses *tú* (informal "you") on the web for approachability, but no slang (Hypothesis; confirm with the founder at copy stage); Catalan must be native quality (not machine-literal); English for international residents avoids local jargon or explains it.

## 09. Boundaries

**Never communicate:** cheapest, best, cleanest or any superlative; fixed price or completion date before inspection; permits included by default; guarantee or insurance before confirmed; Barcelona projects, reviews, years or counts that don't exist; inspiration images as completed work; energy-saving amounts; urgency, scarcity or discounts; "sin IVA" or cash deals. (Decision — 00 §8, 04 §4)

**Concepts to avoid:** (1) "renovation nightmare" fear-selling; (2) luxury prestige or exclusivity.

**Perceptions to avoid:** discount or bargain firm; pushy sales operation; cold technical contractor; foreign newcomer without roots (address it through honesty and a local presence, not by hiding it).

## 10. Creative Principles

- **Campaigns:** (1) Every ad makes one concrete, provable point (a cost line, a standard, a step), never a superlative. (2) The landing page must continue the exact promise of the ad (Google or Instagram) with no bait price.
- **Content:** (1) Show, don't claim: every promise links to a document, step or standard. (2) Pair each precise detail with its human meaning ("Sockets taped and covered, so you can keep living there").
- **Storytelling:** (1) Stories are about the client's home and choices, not about the company's greatness. (2) Only real projects become case stories; until then, explain the process and label concepts as concepts.
