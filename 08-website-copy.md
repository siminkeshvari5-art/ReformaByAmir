# Website copy context

## Scope and source files
One page in three languages (ES default, CA, EN) plus three legal stub pages. Sources: 00, 02, 04, 06, 07, `assets/brand/visual-direction.md`, `assets/brand/Amir.PNG`.

## Priority reader and arrival context
An owner improving or partly renovating the flat they live in, in Putxet, Gràcia or Sant Gervasi, arriving on a phone from Google or Instagram and comparing firms and prices. They are deciding whether to ask for a visit.

## Message strategy and hierarchy
Core idea: *your budget shapes the choices, not the quality of the work, and you see every cost.* Order: purpose → friction → one standard → open ledger → estimate → process → services → who → fit and objections → request.

## Voice and terminology
Clear, specific, calm. Short sentences; no superlatives, urgency or fear-selling. Spanish uses *tú*. "IVA" is kept in all languages. "Our margin / nuestro margen / el nostre marge" is always named explicitly.

## Claims, proof and prohibited language
- **Allowed (process commitments):** every cost shown in four lines; written scope; written change approval; payment stages set in writing; reply within 24 h; same standards list; permits guided with responsibility agreed in writing.
- **Qualified:** estimator = preliminary, based on typical Barcelona prices, not a quote; programme = realistic, not guaranteed.
- **Prohibited:** cheapest, best or cleanest; fixed price or date before a visit; permits included; guarantee, insurance or showroom; client counts, reviews, years; energy-saving figures; urgency or discounts; third-party images as own work.

## CTA and conversion path
Header and S01 "Request a free visit" → S10 form → form service → reformabyamir@gmail.com (**Connection required before launch**) → success or error → one reply within 24 h by the chosen method → visit → itemised quote. Alternatives: WhatsApp +34 661 663 335, email. The estimator result can be attached to the request.
